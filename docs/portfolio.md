# Portfolio Copy

## One-line project description

Built an explainable, revenue-aware telecom churn intelligence platform that combines calibrated Random Forest predictions, SHAP explanations, risk segmentation, revenue-at-risk estimation, and retention prioritization.

## Resume bullets

- Built a calibrated Random Forest churn framework achieving 0.8208 ROC-AUC, 0.6069 PR-AUC, and 0.6497 recall on a held-out telecom customer test set.
- Improved probability quality through sigmoid calibration, reducing Brier score from 0.1562 to 0.1431 and log loss from 0.4768 to 0.4397.
- Developed revenue-aware retention prioritization using calibrated churn probability and expected monthly revenue exposure, with $27,257.11 total expected monthly revenue at risk in the evaluation population.
- Translated customer risk into practical retention segments and targeting scenarios, including 34.49% churner capture from the High-risk group covering 13.13% of evaluated customers.
