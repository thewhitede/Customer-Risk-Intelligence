# CV / Portfolio Project Bullets

## Customer Risk Intelligence Platform

- Built an explainable telecom churn intelligence framework combining Random Forest classification, probability calibration, SHAP explainability, risk segmentation, revenue-at-risk estimation, and retention prioritization.
- Achieved 0.8208 ROC-AUC, 0.6069 PR-AUC, and 0.6497 recall on the established held-out research evaluation.
- Improved probability quality through sigmoid calibration, reducing Brier score from 0.1562 to 0.1431 and log loss from 0.4768 to 0.4397.
- Designed revenue-aware retention prioritization that exposed $27,257.11 expected monthly revenue risk in the evaluation population and supported focused targeting scenarios.
